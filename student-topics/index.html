Chaotic cryptography on FPGA

Areas: Cryptography, nonlinear systems, FPGA

Secure and Trustworthy Medical AI

Dr. Kim-Hue Ta

Access control for Fog-based IoMT

Explore multi-authority ciphertext-policy attribute-based encryption (CP-ABE) and zero-knowledge proofs of attribute ownership for sharing medical records across hospitals.

Areas: Access control, CP-ABE, zero-knowledge proofs

Contact: hue.tathikim@hust.edu.vn

DICOM image integrity

Study reversible embedding in non-diagnostic image borders with chaotic scrambling and Groth16 zk-SNARK verification to detect tampering.

Areas: Medical data integrity, steganography, zk-SNARKs

Contact: hue.tathikim@hust.edu.vn

Verifiable clinical AI inference (zkML)

Prove that a declared model produced a clinical result while protecting patient data and model weights. Explore VOLE-based proofs and links to clinical evidence.

Areas: Zero-knowledge machine learning, clinical AI

Contact: hue.tathikim@hust.edu.vn

Post-quantum security for medical data and 5G

Explore lattice-based CP-ABE/ZKP and hybrid ML-KEM/ML-DSA with physical-layer keys admitted only after verification tests.

Areas: Post-quantum cryptography, physical-layer security

Contact: hue.tathikim@hust.edu.vn

Model lineage and LLM provenance

Study model lineage and detector evasion. Explore watermarking, provenance auditing and private LLM inference.

Areas: Trustworthy AI, provenance, model security

Contact: hue.tathikim@hust.edu.vn

These directions include ongoing work under review, work in preparation and exploratory studies. Model lineage and LLM provenance is an early-stage direction open to students.

LLMs, LVLMs and Edge AI

Dr. Tien-Cuong Bui

Trustworthy LLM and LVLM judges

Check model answers against text, images and video. Compare judging methods using accuracy, agreement with human labels and calibration.

Areas: LLM evaluation, visual question answering, grounded RAG

Contact: cuong.buitien@hust.edu.vn

Video event understanding with LVLMs

Combine detection, tracking and temporal reasoning to retrieve and verify video events, such as a bag left behind.

Areas: Video retrieval, tracking, temporal reasoning

Contact: cuong.buitien@hust.edu.vn

Multimodal agents for smart monitoring

Build agents that check camera, sensor and log evidence before sending alerts or escalating to a human reviewer.

Areas: Multimodal agents, monitoring, tool use

Contact: cuong.buitien@hust.edu.vn

Efficient LVLM/VLA inference on Jetson

Schedule vision and language models on Jetson devices. Explore adaptive visual tokens, multiple camera streams and real-time control.

Areas: Edge AI, embedded inference, scheduling

Contact: cuong.buitien@hust.edu.vn
